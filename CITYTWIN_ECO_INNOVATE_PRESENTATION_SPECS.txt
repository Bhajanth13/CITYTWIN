1. Macroscopic Traffic PDE (Lighthill-Whitham-Richards Conservation):
   ∂k/∂t + ∂(k · v)/∂x = 0
   • Preserves vehicle conservation along the corridor; prevents artificial vehicle loss.
   • Grounded in LWR traffic flow theory, according to the Proceedings of the Royal Society (1955).

2. Greenshields Non-Linear Velocity Curve:
   v(k) = v_free · [1 - (k / k_jam)^1.2]
   • Calibrated parameters: v_free = 55 km/h, k_jam = 120 veh/km, v_floor = 6 km/h.
   • Captures real urban congestion where average rush-hour speeds drop to 16–18 km/h, according to the TomTom Traffic Index.

3. Bureau of Public Roads (BPR) Link Delay Function:
   T_e = T_0 · [1 + 0.20 · (V_e / C_e)^3.5]
   • Captures exponential delay explosion once link volume surpasses physical capacity, according to the Highway Capacity Manual.

[EXACT BREAKDOWN: HOW SIMULINK IS USED IN THIS PROJECT]:
1. Dynamic State-Space Plant Modeling:
   • The 'Continuous/Integrator' block continuously solves the Lighthill-Whitham-Richards (LWR) conservation PDE: k(t) = ∫[(q_in - q_out)/L] dt.
2. Closed-Loop PID Signal Control:
   • Feedback error e(t) = k_target - k(t) feeds the PID Controller block (Kp=0.018, Ki=0.006, Kd=0.002) to dynamically adjust intersection green-time duty cycle u_green(t).
3. Disturbance Injection:
   • A 'Sources/Step' block injects an accident at t = 25s, reducing capacity by 70% to test system transient stability.
4. Physical Saturation Constraints:
   • A 'Discontinuities/Saturation' block constrains green duty cycle to [0.10, 0.90] to prevent cross-traffic starvation.
5. Real-Time Oscilloscope Waveforms:
   • 'Sinks/Scope' blocks display real-time waveforms for Vehicle Density k(t), Average Velocity v(t), and EPA AQI.

[EXACT BREAKDOWN: HOW MATLAB IS USED IN THIS PROJECT]:
1. Graph-Theory Routing Engine (emergency_dijkstra_routing.m):
   • Builds an 18-node directed city graph using `digraph()` and solves emergency shortest paths with dynamic BPR edge weights using `shortestpath()`.
2. Spatial Atmospheric Diffusion (spatial_aqi_dispersion.m):
   • Solves 2D/3D Gaussian plume dispersion matrices over a 10 km x 10 km grid using `meshgrid()`, `contourf()`, `quiver()`, and `surf()`.
3. Multi-Objective Decision Optimizer (pareto_decision_optimizer.m):
   • Evaluates 3D Pareto frontier dominance vectors and executes 120 Monte Carlo perturbation runs using `scatter3()` and matrix algebra.
4. Programmatic Automation & Web Bridge (CITYTWIN_MASTER_SUITE.m):
   • Automates simulation runs via `sim()`, extracts logged signals, and exports `citytwin_matlab_telemetry.json` to bridge MATLAB directly to our 3D WebGL Digital Twin.
